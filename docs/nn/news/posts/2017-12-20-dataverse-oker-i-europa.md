# Dataverse øker i Europa

AUSSDA – The Austrian Social Science Data Archive har nå tatt i bruk Dataverse som plattform. 
 «Our dataverse installation is now live and we are beginning the process of migrating about 700 old Nesstar DDI2.5 records into our new platform ( [data.aussda.at](https://data.aussda.at/) ).»
