# People

DataverseNO is supported by a national network of people working with research data support, curation, collection management, repository operation, policy development, and governance.

On this page, you can learn more about the people involved in DataverseNO and find colleagues with relevant roles, institutional connections, and areas of expertise. The overview includes support staff at partner institutions, members of collection management teams, curators, repository management, board members, and others who contribute to developing and maintaining DataverseNO as a trusted repository for open research data.
