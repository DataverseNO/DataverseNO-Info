# Deposit Guidelines

Want to publish and share your research data through DataverseNO? Most datasets can be prepared by following a few simple steps, and support is available throughout the process.

The purpose of these guidelines is to help you organize and document your data in a way that supports reuse and long-term preservation. The guidelines explain how to (1) prepare your data, (2) deposit them in the repository as a dataset, (3) publish it, (4) refer to it and, if needed, (5) modify your dataset after it is published.

## Before you get started

Before you begin preparing your dataset, there are a few important questions and answers to consider. If you are unsure about terms such as DOI, metadata, embargo, curation, or dataset, see the [PAGE: glossary/index | Glossary].

### Can I use DataverseNO to publish and share my data?

DataverseNO supports research data from all disciplines and is free to use for researchers affiliated with Norwegian research institutions. Read more about who can share data in DataverseNO on the [PAGE: about/who-can-use-dataverseno#sharing-data-in-dataverseno | about page].

### What if my data are sensitive?

DataverseNO currently only accepts data that can be openly shared. If your data requires protection, certain steps or considerations may be necessary before they can be shared openly. If you are unsure what applies to your dataset, please contact your [REUSE: links/contact-page | local user support].

### Will I get help to publish my data?

Support is available throughout the process. All datasets submitted to DataverseNO are reviewed by trained data curators before publication. Curators do not evaluate the scientific quality of the research itself but can help improve documentation, metadata, file organization, file formats, and licensing information. You do not need to get everything perfect from the start. Read more about curation on the [PAGE: about/why-use-dataverseno#curation-and-preservation | about page].

### What if I need restricted access?

DataverseNO currently does not offer restricted file access. It is possible to specify an embargo period during which the embargoed files will not be accessible, but the dataset metadata will remain visible. The embargo period is temporary and will end automatically at a specified end date, maximum [REUSE: values/embargo-period] after publication. Read more about applying an embargo in [PAGE: deposit/deposit-your-data#embargo-period | Step 2: Deposit your data].

### Will my dataset become citable?

Published datasets receive a [REUSE: terms/digital-object-identifier], making them citable and easier to discover and reuse. Read more about dataset visibility on the [PAGE: about/why-use-dataverseno#credit-and-visibility | about page] or learn how to cite datasets in the [PAGE: deposit/refer-to-your-data | deposit guidelines].

### Can I modify my dataset after publication?

Yes, it is possible to modify your dataset after publication. Doing so will create a new version of the dataset. Read more about versioning on the page [PAGE: deposit/modify-your-data].
