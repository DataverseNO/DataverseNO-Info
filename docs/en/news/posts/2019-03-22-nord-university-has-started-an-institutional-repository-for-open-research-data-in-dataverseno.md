# Nord University has started an institutional repository for open research data in DataverseNO

[DataverseNO](https://dataverse.no/) welcomes **Nord University** as a partner institution with an institutional repository – [NORD Open research Data](https://dataverse.no/dataverse/nord) – for archiving and sharing open research data. This will give researchers at Nord University possibilities for increased impact and citations of their published datasets and strengthen the open science skills at the institution.
