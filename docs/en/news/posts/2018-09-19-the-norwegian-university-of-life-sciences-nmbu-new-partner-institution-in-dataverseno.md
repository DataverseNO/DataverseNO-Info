# The Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU) new partner institution in DataverseNO

UiT welcomes NMBU as a partner institution of [DataverseNO](https://dataverse.no/). We are looking forward to cooperating with one of the leading research institutions in Norway on archiving and sharing open research data from HE institutions in Norway.
