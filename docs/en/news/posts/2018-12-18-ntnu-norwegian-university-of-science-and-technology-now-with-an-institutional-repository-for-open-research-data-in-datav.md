# NTNU – Norwegian University of Science and Technology now with an institutional repository for open research data in DataverseNO

[DataverseNO](https://dataverse.no/) welcomes NTNU as a partner institution with an institutional repository – [NTNU Open Research Data](https://dataverse.no/dataverse/ntnu) – for archiving and sharing open research data. This will give researchers at NTNU possibilities for increased impact and citations of their published datasets and strengthen the open science skills at the institution.
