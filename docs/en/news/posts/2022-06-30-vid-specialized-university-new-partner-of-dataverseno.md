# VID Specialized University new partner of DataverseNO

DataverseNO welcomes VID Specialized University as a partner institution. With its own institutional collection ( [https://dataverse.no/dataverse/vid](https://dataverse.no/dataverse/vid) ) in DataverseNO, VID can now offer their researchers a certified and curated data repository where they can make their data openly available and thus make their research more visible, and promote competence on open research.
