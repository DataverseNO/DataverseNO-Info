# Western Norway University of Applied Sciences (HVL) has started an institutional repository for open research data in DataverseNO

[DataverseNO](https://dataverse.no/) welcomes HVL as a partner institution with an institutional repository– [HVL Open Research Data](https://dataverse.no/dataverse/hvl) – for archiving and sharing open research data. This will give researchers at HVL possibilities for increased impact and citations of their published datasets and strengthen the open science skills at the institution.
